# Urgency rubric: council complaints

Every labelled case gets one band from 1 to 5. Pick the highest band whose test is met. The band is about risk to people, not about how angry or formal the letter sounds.

| Band | Name | Test | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Emergency | Risk to life or safety right now, or someone has nowhere to sleep tonight. Act today. | Ceiling down with live electrics; family with children homeless tonight |
| 4 | Urgent | Serious harm likely within days; or a vulnerable person (young child, older person living alone, disabled or seriously ill person) harmed by an ongoing failure; or a hazard that has already injured someone and is still present. Act within 2 working days. | No heating with a baby in cold weather; mould worsening a child's asthma; an unrepaired pothole that has already caused an injury |
| 3 | High | A significant failure that is ongoing or repeated, with no injury so far and no immediate danger. | Broken paving where people have tripped but nobody was hurt; months of night-time noise |
| 2 | Standard | A routine service failure or request with no health or safety impact. | Missed bins; wrong council tax discount; a disputed parking ticket |
| 1 | Low | Minor feedback or a suggestion. | Wrong opening hours on a web page; a wobbly park bench |

Rules of thumb:
- Strong words like "urgent" or "distressing" do not raise the band on their own.
- Citing laws does not raise the band.
- A vulnerable person raises the band only if the failure actually affects them.
- **Deciding factor between 3 and 4 for a hazard: has it already injured someone, and is it still there?** If both are yes, it is band 4. A trip with no injury, or an injury from a hazard that has since been fixed, stays at band 3.

## Contested labels

Some cases sit on the line between two bands. Their label has `contested: true` and the notes explain both sides. Any urgency evaluation (Win 2 onwards) should report both **exact accuracy** and **within-one-band accuracy**, so a 3 vs 4 judgement call is not scored the same as a 1 vs 5 mistake.
